A quiet place to read, learn and grow.

Readers' Haven is the city's public library, open to every resident. Borrow from over 40,000 books, use our study rooms, or browse e-books from home.

Our Mission

Since 1998, Readers' Haven has offered free and low-cost access to books, information and learning for everyone in the city, whatever their age or background.

We run reading programmes for children, provide quiet study space for students, and offer digital services so that residents can keep reading wherever they are.
