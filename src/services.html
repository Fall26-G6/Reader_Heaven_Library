Digital Services

Search the catalogue, read e-books, book a study room, and print or scan your documents, all in one place.

🔎

Online Catalogue

Available 24/7 Free

Search all 40,000+ titles and check whether a book is on the shelf before you visit.

  1. Go to the catalogue terminal or the online portal.
  2. Search by title, author, subject or ISBN.
  3. Note the shelf number, or place a hold with your member ID.
📱

E-Books & Audiobooks

Available 24/7 Adult & Family plans

Borrow from 6,500 e-books and 800 audiobooks on your phone, tablet or laptop.

  1. Log in to the e-library with your membership number.
  2. Borrow up to 5 titles at a time for 14 days.
  3. Titles return automatically, so there are no late fees.
🪑

Study-Room Booking

Mon–Sat, 9 AM – 7 PM 12 rooms

Quiet rooms for 1–6 people, each with a whiteboard and power outlets.

  1. Book up to 3 days ahead at the front desk or by phone.
  2. Each booking is for up to 2 hours.
  3. Check in within 15 minutes of your start time, or the room is released.
🖨️

Printing & Scanning

During opening hours Ground floor

Self-service printers and scanners at the Digital Desk.

  • Black & white print: PKR 10 per page
  • Colour print: PKR 30 per page
  • Scanning to email or USB: free for members
📶

Free Wi-Fi

During opening hours Free

Fast wireless internet across all three floors for members and visitors.

  1. Connect to the ReadersHaven-Guest network.
  2. Enter your member ID, or your mobile number to receive a one-time code.
  3. Each session lasts 3 hours and can be renewed.
💻

Computer Lab

Mon–Sat, 10 AM – 6 PM 20 PCs

Desktop computers with office software and internet access for research and assignments.

  1. Sign in at the lab desk with your membership card.
  2. Use a computer for up to 1 hour, or longer if no one is waiting.
Need help? Staff at the Digital Desk on the ground floor can help with any of these services during opening hours. You can also send us a message.
