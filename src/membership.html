Membership Plans

Choose the plan that suits you. All memberships last one year and include free Wi-Fi and online catalogue access.

Student

PKR 1,000 / year

For school, college and university students with a valid student ID.

  • Borrowing limit: 3 books at a time
  • Loan period: 14 days
  • Renewals: 1 renewal per book
  • Study room: 2 hours per day
Most Popular

Adult

PKR 2,500 / year

For residents aged 18 and above. Includes full e-book access.

  • Borrowing limit: 5 books at a time
  • Loan period: 21 days
  • Renewals: 2 renewals per book
  • E-books: 5 at a time

Family

PKR 4,000 / year

Up to 2 adults and 3 children living at the same address.

  • Borrowing limit: 12 books in total
  • Loan period: 21 days
  • Renewals: 2 renewals per book
  • Kids' events: Free entry

Late-Return Rules

  • A 2-day grace period applies before any late fee is charged.
  • Late fees are charged per book, per day, as shown in the table above.
  • The late fee for one book will not exceed PKR 500.
  • Borrowing is paused while unpaid fees are more than PKR 300.
  • A book more than 30 days late counts as lost, and its replacement cost is charged.

How to Join

  1. Visit the front desk with your CNIC (or B-Form for children).
  2. Students should also bring a valid student ID card.
  3. Fill in the membership form and pay the annual fee.
  4. Collect your membership card. You can start borrowing the same day.
